# Ship 30 for 30 Atomic Essay Skill

## Overview
The **Ship 30 for 30 Atomic Essay Skill** transforms podcast transcript knowledge into structured, high-signal, ~1,250-word atomic essays designed for rapid executive consumption and high engagement.

## Core Structural Rules & Principles

1. **The Atomic Hook (First 3 Seconds)**
   - Must open with a bold, non-obvious assertion or provocative question.
   - Immediately frame why conventional wisdom fails.
   - Example: *"Most startup founders treat Product-Market Fit like lightning in a bottle. You either get struck, or you wander in the dark until your bank balance hits zero."*

2. **1-3-1 Sentence Cadence (Visual Rhythm & Anti-Wall-of-Text)**
   - Sentence 1: Short punchy claim (1 line).
   - Paragraph: 2–3 sentences elaborating on the core tension or mechanism.
   - Sentence 3: Short punchy takeaway (1 line).
   - Prevents reading fatigue on both mobile and desktop screens.

3. **Subheadings & Bold Scannability**
   - Use descriptive, action-oriented H3 headers (e.g. `### The 40% Leading Indicator That Changes Everything`).
   - Highlight core keywords and numbers in **bold** for readers skimming the essay in under 30 seconds.

4. **Transcript Evidence & Specificity**
   - Ground the framework in concrete numbers, exact guest anecdotes, and operational steps from Lenny's Podcast.
   - Avoid generic AI platitudes ("In today's fast-paced digital world...").

5. **The One Big Takeaway (Final Section)**
   - Must conclude with an explicit **The Big Takeaway** section in 2 actionable sentences summarizing the operational framework.

## Output Format
- Title: `# Title: High-Impact Concept`
- Word count: ~1,000 – 1,250 words
- Saved as an interactive markdown artifact in the side-by-side workspace.
